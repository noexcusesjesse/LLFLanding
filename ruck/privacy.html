Privacy

The R.U.C.K. Foundation is being formed as an Arizona nonprofit. 501(c)(3) status has not been applied for or granted.

This notice covers the interest list on the foundation page. The contact path, while the foundation is in formation, is that form.

What the form collects

The interest form collects only your name, email, ZIP code, and the interests you check: walks, board, scholarships, and the gear program. It does not ask health questions.

How the foundation uses it

The data is used only by the R.U.C.K. Foundation, to tell you when a planned program launches. It is never shared with or used by LoadLine or No Excuses.

Submissions are stored in a separate table named ruck_interest. They are not written into the website leads list, and they are not added to any marketing email.

Deletion

Your information is deleted on request. Use the form below with the same name and email you submitted. Matching rows are removed from the foundation list.
