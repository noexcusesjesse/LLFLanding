R.U.C.K. Foundation

Lace up. Rise up. Keep walking.

This page is an introduction while the foundation is being formed. Nothing here is open for enrollment, and this page does not collect money.

Join the list

The R.U.C.K. Foundation is being formed as an Arizona nonprofit. 501(c)(3) status has not been applied for or granted.

Mission

The mission is to help and motivate people toward a healthy lifestyle.

A healthy lifestyle can be a walk, a ride, or time spent moving with other people. The foundation wants to help people get started and keep going.

Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

Planned programs

Each program below is planned, not active. Joining the interest list does not enroll you, hold a place, or start an application.

Planned, not active

G.R.I.T. (working name) trainer certification scholarships

G.R.I.T. (working name) is a planned scholarship program for people who want to become certified trainers. It is not open. This page has no scholarship application, and no award amount is set.

Planned, not active

Shoes, socks, and movement clothing

A planned program would provide running, walking, and cycling shoes, socks, and movement clothing for kids, teens, college students, working parents, and seniors. Distribution would use income-based tiers and would put underserved areas first. The tiers are not set, and nothing is available to request yet.

Planned, not active

Community walks

Free and paid community walks are planned. Mesa's Signal Butte Park is the preferred venue. No walk is on the calendar yet.

Call 911 first in an emergency. Stop walking if you feel chest pain, faintness, or severe shortness of breath.

Planned, not active

Walking Wonderland (working name)

Walking Wonderland (working name) is a long-term idea for a park and trail. It is not a site you can visit, and it is not under construction.

Founding board

The foundation is recruiting 5 volunteer seats for a founding board. Asking to be considered does not appoint you.

Board seats are volunteer. Reasonable, approved expenses are reimbursed. Board pay may be considered in the future, subject to legal and financial review.
