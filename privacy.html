What we collect

We collect only what the forms and accounts below ask for. If a field is optional, we store it only when you fill it in.

Plan Builder

When you request a plan at Plan Builder, we store:

  • Your first name
  • Your email
  • Your marketing consent choice, saved as yes or no
  • The time you acknowledged the disclaimer
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older
  • The source page, saved as plan-builder

Home page contact form

The contact form on the home page sends us:

  • Your name
  • Your email
  • Your phone number, if you add one
  • Your message

We save that request as a contact lead.

Tracker access request

The tracker at /tracker can take a request for access. That request includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email
  • Your phone number, if you add one
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older

Tracker account and logs

A tracker account stores:

  • Username
  • Email
  • Password, saved as a hash rather than the password you typed
  • Display name
  • The confirmation, from signup, that the person is 18 or older

While you are signed in, the tracker also saves the record you keep there. That record can include:

  • Profile: name, gender, age, height, weight, waist, and eating window
  • Body-measurement log: date, weight, waist, and notes
  • Daily entries you choose to log: workouts and sets, meals and protein, carbs, and fat, water, sleep, supplement checks, steps, daily primer checks, grocery checks, and challenges

A copy of that record stays in the browser. When you are signed in, the site also copies it to our database. Your sign-in token is kept in the browser's local storage, not in a cookie, and it expires after 30 days.

What stays in your browser

Plan Builder body metrics and the schedule stay in your browser and are never sent to us. That includes your goal, experience, workout days, daily step goal, weight, height, age, sex, waist, wake time, work start, work end, bedtime, eating-window start, and equipment. They are used only to build the plan on your device, including the PDF you download there.

A few other tools also stay on the device:

  • The home page can remember, in that browser only, the time of your first visit, how many times you have come back, the last section you reached, and your answers to the on-page quiz. Those notes are not sent to us.
  • Walkingout saves your routine in that browser: wake time, work hours, lunch, breaks, bedtime, days off, step goal, everyday steps, and the step totals you log. There is no Walkingout account, and LoadLine cannot see that routine.
  • WalkQuest saves a trail name, distance units, stride settings, height if you enter it, the campaigns you start, and the miles, steps, and notes you log. WalkQuest does not send that record to LoadLine.

Walkingout and WalkQuest can keep a copy of the app in the browser so it opens again faster. That copy stays on your device. Walkingout can show a reminder only while the page is open, and only if you allow notifications. We do not receive those reminders.

Health information

Tracker logs, including weight, waist, meals, sleep, and supplement checks, are used only to run your tracker. They are never used for marketing, never sold or shared, and are deleted on request. LoadLine Fitness is not a medical provider, and these logs are not medical records.

How we use it

We use your Plan Builder name and email to send you the plan. We send marketing emails, including tips and offers, only if you opt in. Plan emails and marketing emails are sent with Gmail (Google). You can download the plan in your browser either way.

We use a contact-form message to reply to you. We use a tracker access request to review access and to contact you about it. We use a tracker account and the logs you save to run the tracker for you.

How to unsubscribe

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe by emailing noexcusesjesse@gmail.com.

Who receives the data

Leads are stored only in the site's own leads database. Two services outside LoadLine Fitness receive information:

  • Railway hosts the website and the PostgreSQL database.
  • Google, through Gmail, sends the plan and any marketing emails. Google receives the names and email addresses on those messages.

We do not use a CRM. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with any other third party. We will update this policy before any other vendor is added.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use an analytics product, and it does not set analytics cookies or advertising pixels. Pages do not set a cookie for tracking.

The browser's local storage holds the home-page visit note, Walkingout, WalkQuest, the tracker sign-in token, a local copy of tracker logs, and the staff dashboard sign-in token. Those are stored in the browser. They are not cookies.

Retention, deletion, and access

Leads who never opted in to marketing are deleted after 12 months. We keep other information we store for as long as we need it to reply to you, send what you asked for, and run a tracker account.

Email noexcusesjesse@gmail.com to ask what we have, to ask for a copy, to ask for a correction, or to ask us to delete it. We will reply to deletion requests within 30 days. Deleting a tracker account removes its logs from our database.

Information that lives only in your browser stays there until you clear site data for this site. We cannot delete that copy for you.

Ages

This site is not for people under 18. Plan Builder, tracker signup, and the tracker access request each ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older before you continue. If you believe we have information about someone under 18, email us and ask us to delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we store. Tracker passwords are stored as hashes. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, stolen, or changed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date.

California and other state rights

If you live in California, or in another state with similar privacy rights, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of a sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To use any of these rights, email noexcusesjesse@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you. This is a general description, not a full statement of every state law.
