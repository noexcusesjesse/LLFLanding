Protein First
Start every meal with a protein source. Aim for 0.7 to 1 gram per pound of your target bodyweight daily. This single change drives everything else.
2
Walk After You Eat
A 10 to 15-minute walk after your largest meal blunts blood sugar spikes and improves insulin sensitivity. No gym required. Just move.
3
Sleep Is the Multiplier
Everything works better when you sleep 7 to 9 hours. Set a consistent bedtime, dim the lights, and put the phone away 30 minutes before bed.
