# Fasting Safety

**General wellness and education. Not medical advice.** Talk with your doctor before you change how you eat, fast, or exercise.

## 1. Where fasting happens

LoadLine Solo and LoadLine 365 do not use fasting. There are no fasting days or fasting windows in either program, and fasting is not part of Basic Training.

Fasting shows up in only two places:

- **The free No Excuses Challenges** (Run by No Excuses Jesse, Powered by LoadLine Fitness). These are education, not a program. LoadLine does not supervise any fast.
- **The No Excuses Reset Program,** with strict limits. See Section 4.

## 2. Doctor checks by fast length (challenges)

- **12, 16, or 20 hours:** talk to your doctor before you fast.
- **24 hours:** your doctor must tell you a 24-hour fast is OK for you.
- **Over 24 hours:** all three boxes of the doctor-cleared check-in are required. **36 hours is the maximum.**

**The doctor-cleared check-in (all three boxes, required for anything over 24 hours):**

- [ ] I am 18 or older.
- [ ] I have completed a 24-hour fast without problems. (Any 24-hour fast you've done counts.)
- [ ] My doctor has told me this specific fast is OK for me.

The longest option is a choice, not a dare. A 12-hour window done right counts the same as anything longer.

Challenge dates, lengths, and rules live at [noexcuseschallenges.com](https://noexcuseschallenges.com).

## 3. LoadLine members and challenge fasts

- **In Basic Training?** Skip all challenge fasts, at every length.
- **Enrolled in LoadLine Solo or LoadLine 365?** You can join any free No Excuses Challenge. To join a challenge fast, you must have finished Basic Training and your doctor must have cleared you for fasting. Clearance for exercise doesn't count. The doctor checks in Section 2 still apply.

## 4. No Excuses Reset Program

The No Excuses Reset Program runs 15 months, plus Basic Training before it starts.

- **24-hour fasts and 36-hour fasts** are the only longer fasts in the program. 36 hours is the longest.
- Both need your physician's clearance first. The 36-hour fast also needs all three boxes of the doctor-cleared check-in in Section 2. No clearance, no fast.
- Neither one is ever used in **Basic Training** or **The Ninety** (the first three months).

## 5. Who should not fast

Every fast on this page, in challenges and in the Reset Program, is for adults 18 and older.

**Do not fast if you:**

- are under 18
- are pregnant or nursing
- have a current or past eating disorder

**Don't fast unless your doctor has cleared you for that specific fast if you:**

- have diabetes or low blood sugar
- take any prescription medicine, especially for blood sugar or blood pressure
- have a heart or kidney condition

## 6. Medicine

LoadLine does not prescribe, dose, or adjust any medicine, before, during, or after a fast. If you take any medicine, talk to your own doctor before any fast.

If you take medicine for blood sugar and feel shaky, sweaty, dizzy, or confused, stop the fast, follow the plan your doctor gave you, and call your doctor. Call 911 if it's severe.

## 7. Low blood sugar warning signs

Low blood sugar can look like:

- shakiness
- sweating
- a pounding heart
- confusion
- feeling faint

If that happens, stop the fast and eat or drink something with sugar now. If you don't feel better soon, or you get confused or can't keep food down, call 911.

## 8. When to call 911

Call 911 for:

- chest pain or pressure
- fainting
- confusion that doesn't clear
- seizure
- a racing or irregular heartbeat
- severe shortness of breath

## 9. Stopping early

Any early stop counts as complete. Longer fasts don't guarantee any result. There are no prizes for hours.
