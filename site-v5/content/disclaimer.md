**Effective date:** November 2, 2026 · Version 2.3

This disclaimer and consent applies to anyone who uses LoadLine Solo or LoadLine 365 from LoadLine Fitness ("LoadLine," "we," "us"). You agree to it by checking the boxes in the Acknowledgment and Consent and submitting the LoadLine Intake Questionnaire with your typed name (online) or your signature (printable copy).

**You must be 18 or older.** LoadLine Solo and LoadLine 365 are for adults only.

## 2. Wellness Program, Not Medical Care

LoadLine is a wellness and lifestyle program. It is not medical care.

- LoadLine does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.
- LoadLine does not prescribe diets, calorie targets, medicines, supplements, or surgery, and does not give advice about any medicine. Questions about weight-loss medicines or weight-loss surgery are for your doctor.
- LoadLine never prescribes, doses, or adjusts any medicine.
- In LoadLine 365, your accountability partner, mentor and motivator is **not** a doctor, registered dietitian, physical therapist, nurse, or mental health professional, and does not act as one.
- Nothing in the program replaces advice from your own doctor or other licensed professional. If anything in LoadLine conflicts with what your doctor tells you, follow your doctor.

## 3. Talk to Your Doctor Before Starting

Talk to your doctor before you start. The Intake Questionnaire includes a health screen modeled on the American College of Sports Medicine's pre-participation screening approach. It is not a medical exam.

- If the screen says to see your doctor first, do that before starting movement or food changes. Until you are cleared, skip the chair check (standing up from a chair and sitting back down for 30 seconds) and the walking check (timing how long you can walk before you need to stop).
- If you have diabetes, kidney disease, high blood pressure, or take medicine for your heart, blood sugar, or kidneys, talk to your doctor before you change how you eat or move.
- If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have had recent surgery, use a weight-loss medicine, or have had weight-loss surgery, follow your medical team's instructions first.
- If you have a history of an eating disorder or current concerns about your eating, get the OK from your doctor or a mental health professional before any food changes.

## 4. Warning Signs and Emergencies

Stop all activity and get medical help right away if you have chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to your arm, neck, jaw, or back, extreme shortness of breath, fainting, or dizziness. **Call 911 in an emergency.**

If you take medicine for blood sugar and feel shaky, sweaty, dizzy, or confused, follow the plan your doctor gave you and call your doctor. Call 911 if it's severe.

If you are in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, call, text, or chat **988** (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call **911** if you are in immediate danger.

LoadLine is not an emergency service. Do not use LoadLine messages, check-ins, or calls to report an emergency.

## 5. No Promised Results

**Individual results vary. LoadLine does not promise or guarantee any specific weight, size, health, or fitness result, or any timeline.** Any examples in LoadLine materials are for illustration only and are not predictions. Research findings describe groups of people, not what will happen for any one person. Your results depend on many things, including your health, history, and circumstances.

## 6. Fasting

LoadLine Solo and LoadLine 365 do not use fasting. There are no fasting days or fasting windows in either program, and fasting is not part of Basic Training.

Fasting happens only in the free No Excuses Challenges (Run by No Excuses Jesse, Powered by LoadLine Fitness). They are education, not part of either program, and LoadLine does not supervise any fast. A LoadLine Solo or LoadLine 365 member may join a challenge fast only after finishing Basic Training, and only if their doctor has cleared them for fasting. Clearance for exercise doesn't count.

- **12, 16, or 20 hours:** the open challenge windows. Talk to your doctor before you fast.
- **24 hours:** needs your doctor's OK for that fast.
- **Over 24 hours:** needs all three boxes of the doctor-cleared check-in. **36 hours is the maximum.** There is no 72-hour fast.

Do not join a challenge fast if you are under 18, pregnant or nursing, or have a current or past eating disorder. If you have diabetes or low blood sugar, take prescription medicine (especially for blood sugar or blood pressure), or have heart or kidney conditions, don't fast unless your doctor has cleared you for that specific fast. LoadLine never prescribes, doses, or adjusts medicine around a fast. If you feel shaky, sweaty, confused, or faint, stop the fast. Call 911 for chest pain or pressure, fainting, confusion that doesn't clear, seizure, a racing or irregular heartbeat, or severe shortness of breath.

## 7. Assumption of Risk

Physical activity, including walking, bodyweight exercise, and resistance-band exercise, carries a risk of injury, including muscle strains, joint pain, falls, and in rare cases serious heart events. Changing how you eat can also affect how you feel. You choose how and when to take part, and you agree to stop if something feels wrong. If you have chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or sudden dizziness, stop right away and call 911. **You take part at your own risk.**

You are responsible for using safe equipment (for example, a sturdy chair rated for your weight and a secure door anchor) and a safe place to move. You are also responsible for listening to your body, going at your own pace, and checking with your doctor if you are unsure whether an activity is right for you.
