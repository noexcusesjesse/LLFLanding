# LoadLine Program Disclaimer and Consent

**Effective date:** Effective on the date posted.

## 1. Who This Covers

This disclaimer and consent applies to anyone who uses LoadLine Solo or LoadLine 365 from LoadLine Fitness ("LoadLine," "we," "us"). By checking the boxes and signing below, or by using either program, you agree to it.

**You must be 18 or older.** LoadLine Solo and LoadLine 365 are for adults only.

## 2. Wellness Program, Not Medical Care

LoadLine is a wellness and lifestyle program. It is not medical care.

- LoadLine does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.
- LoadLine does not prescribe diets, calorie targets, medicines, supplements, or surgery, and does not give advice about any medicine. Questions about weight-loss medicines or weight-loss surgery are for your doctor.
- In LoadLine 365, your accountability partner, mentor and motivator is **not** a doctor, registered dietitian, physical therapist, nurse, or mental health professional, and does not act as one.
- Nothing in the program replaces advice from your own doctor or other licensed professional. If anything in LoadLine conflicts with what your doctor tells you, follow your doctor.

## 3. Talk to Your Doctor Before Starting

Talk to your doctor before you start. The Intake Questionnaire includes a health screen modeled on the American College of Sports Medicine's pre-participation screening approach. It is not a medical exam.

- If the screen says to see your doctor first, do that before starting movement or food changes, and do not do the chair or walking checks until you are cleared.
- If you have diabetes, kidney disease, high blood pressure, or take medicine for your heart, blood sugar, or kidneys, talk to your doctor before you change how you eat or move.
- If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have had recent surgery, use a weight-loss medicine, or have had weight-loss surgery, follow your medical team's instructions first.
- If you have a history of an eating disorder or current concerns about your eating, get the OK from your doctor or a mental health professional before any food changes.

## 4. Warning Signs and Emergencies

Stop all activity and get medical help right away if you have chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to your arm, neck, jaw, or back, extreme shortness of breath, fainting, or dizziness. **Call 911 in an emergency.**

If you are in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, call, text, or chat **988** (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call **911** if you are in immediate danger.

LoadLine is not an emergency service. Do not use LoadLine messages, check-ins, or calls to report an emergency.

## 5. No Promised Results

**Individual results vary. LoadLine does not promise or guarantee any specific weight, size, health, or fitness result, or any timeline.** Any examples in LoadLine materials are for illustration only and are not predictions. Research findings describe groups of people, not what will happen for any one person. Your results depend on many things, including your health, history, and circumstances.

## 6. No Fasting

LoadLine Solo and LoadLine 365 do not use fasting. There are no fasting days or fasting windows in either program.

## 7. Assumption of Risk

Physical activity, including walking, bodyweight exercise, and resistance-band exercise, carries a risk of injury, including muscle strains, joint pain, falls, and in rare cases serious heart events. Changing how you eat can also affect how you feel. You choose how and when to take part, you agree to stop if something feels wrong, and **you take part at your own risk.**

You are responsible for using safe equipment (for example, a sturdy chair rated for your weight and a secure door anchor) and a safe place to move.
