HOME GYM STANDARD

The equipment

LoadLine Solo is built around the HARISON HR-610 Multi-Functional Weight Bench. This is the official residential equipment standard for home clients.

HARISON HR-610 Weight Bench

Compact residential "Fitness Box" all-in-one storage bench. 7-8 lockable multi-angle backrest positions (flat, low incline, 45 degree, upright). Enclosed internal storage for dumbbells, kettlebells, and bands. Built-in base resistance band anchors. Under-frame transport wheels. Dimensions: 43.3 x 12.6 x 45.7 inches. 256 lb user capacity.

FIT-7s Incline Walk Pad

Low-impact incline walking surface for daily step accumulation. Critical for Arizona desert heat protocol when outdoor walking is restricted at 100°F+. Used for the 8,000-step daily floor, warm-ups, and recovery flushes. Pairs with the HR-610 bench for a complete home gym footprint.

Adjustable Dumbbells

5-50 lb adjustable dumbbells for the 3-day resistance split. The LoadLine protocol uses controlled 3-second eccentric temps with ascending tension. No heavy barbells, no ego lifting, no joint compression. Just clean, joint-spared resistance work.

Resistance Bands

Heavy loop bands and handled bands for the wall anchor track system. Ascending elastic tension matches the natural strength curve of your joints, lighter at vulnerable end-range, heaviest at full contraction. Used for rows, pulldowns, face pulls, and terminal knee extensions.
