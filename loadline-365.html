THE PROGRAM

Why LoadLine 365 exists

Most fitness programs sell you 30 days. Some sell you 12 weeks. Then the program ends, the accountability vanishes, and you are right back where you started. That is by design. The fitness industry makes money on your return, not your independence.

LoadLine 365 is different. It is a 12-month periodized curriculum that starts with Boot Camp and ends with NOMAD status, where you can carry the LoadLine standard without needing a coach, gym, or program. The goal is to make you unnecessary. That takes a year, not a month.

Every stage builds on the last. Every quarter has a specific focus and a measurable benchmark. You do not shop the stages. You walk them.

The goal is not to keep you. The goal is to make you independent.

12-MONTH ROADMAP

Four quarters. Four benchmarks.

Each quarter of LoadLine 365 has a specific focus and a measurable benchmark. You know exactly where you are and what comes next.

  • Quarter 1

    Foundation & Metabolic Reset

    Months 1-3. Lock in the 8,000-step baseline, Green Zone weight stabilization, 14:10 TRE rhythm, and drop initial visceral fat.

    Benchmark: 1-Mile Incline Walk Test under 18:00 at 4% incline

  • Quarter 2

    Structural Hypertrophy & Density

    Months 4-6. Progressive overload with dumbbells and heavy band tracks. Drive waist-to-height ratio below 0.48. Build lean tissue.

    Benchmark: 2-Minute Banded Row over 45 strict reps with 2s hold

  • Quarter 3

    Metabolic Shred & Work Capacity

    Months 7-9. Elevated work capacity, Concept2 rower intervals, 10,000-step peak days, and carbohydrate cycling protocols.

    Benchmark: Concept2 500m Sprint under 1:55 split and Zone 2 Cardiac Efficiency Test

  • Quarter 4

    Autonomous Standard (NOMAD Readiness)

    Months 10-12. Complete transition to lifelong independent mastery. You run the standard without being owned by a coach or program.

    Benchmark: 14-Day Uncoached Autonomous Adherence Test and NOMAD 8-Competency Practical Exam
