COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be fit to start?

+

No. LoadLine is built for real people, not athletes. The joint-spared resistance training uses controlled tempos and ascending band tension that scales to any fitness level. Boot Camp is a 14-day onboarding designed to teach you the standard before you start LoadLine 30.

What if I miss a day?

+

Missing a day is normal. Missing a week is a pattern. LoadLine is built on the 7-Day Rolling Average, not daily perfection. One bad day does not erase a week of consistency. The standard is about returning, not being perfect.

Is this a contract?

+

LoadLine 365 is a 12-month curriculum, but you are not locked into a contract. Monthly billing continues until you cancel. The program is designed as a year-long journey to NOMAD status, but life happens. We ask for commitment, not obligation.

What equipment do I need at home?

+

The home gym standard is the HARISON HR-610 Multi-Functional Weight Bench with enclosed storage, a FIT-7s Incline Walk Pad, adjustable dumbbells (5-50 lbs), and resistance bands. The bench stores everything out of sight and rolls aside when you use the walk pad.

How many people train at the studio at once?

+

Maximum 3 members on the training floor at any time, one per station. The 50/10 rule means 50 minutes of private workout execution followed by a mandatory 10-minute wipe-down and equipment reset. This is private coaching, not a crowded gym.

What is NOMAD status?

+

NOMAD is the destination of LoadLine 365. It means you can carry the LoadLine standard anywhere, at home, in a hotel, in a park, or in the facility. You understand nutrition, movement, recovery, and tracking well enough to run the standard without being owned by a coach or program. Independence, not dependency.

Do you work with people outside of Arizona?

+

Yes. The No Excuses Solo tier ($149/mo) is fully remote. You get the HARISON HR-610 home bench standard, LoadLine MOS flight deck access, and the full digital curriculum. We ship the equipment guidance and set you up for success from anywhere.

What makes LoadLine different from a regular gym?

+

Regular gyms sell access. LoadLine teaches ownership. We track 8 clinical domains, not just weight. We use joint-spared resistance training, not ego lifting. We follow the Okinawan 40/30/20/10 plate protocol, not crash diets. And the goal is NOMAD status, where you no longer need us. That is the difference.
